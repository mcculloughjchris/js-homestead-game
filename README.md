# Homesteading under a fascist regime simulator

Thoughts:

- Each route has a room and a direction
  - `/:room/:direction`
  - Example: `/living_room/north` would be in the living room facing north
  - `WASD` to navigate, `WS` to move forward and back, `AD` to turn

- Day cycles
- Household management
  - Answering the front door
  - Eating
  - Sleeping
  - Bathroom use
  - Shotgun cleaning
- Garden management
  - Prepare beds
  - Plant seeds
  - Water plants
- Livestock management
  - Feed
  - Water
  - Clean
  - Process
- Player management
  - Hunger
  - Thirst
  - Energy
  - Money
